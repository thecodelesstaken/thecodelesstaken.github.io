Write to [contact@thecodelesstaken.com](mailto:contact@thecodelesstaken.com) with source material, corrections, collaboration proposals, or general correspondence.

For a correction, link to the page and identify the claim. For historical material, include its origin, date, and any known rights or access restrictions.

System creators, maintainers, long-term practitioners, researchers, archivists, and readers who have reproduced an artifact are especially welcome to get in touch.
