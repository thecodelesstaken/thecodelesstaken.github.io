Write to [contact@thecodelesstaken.com](mailto:contact@thecodelesstaken.com) with primary material, technical experience, reproduction notes, corrections, or collaboration proposals.

For a correction, link to the page and identify the claim. For historical material, include its origin, date, and any known rights or access restrictions.

If you created, maintained, operated, studied, preserved, or reproduced a system covered here, your account can help establish details that manuals and source code do not record.
