This site does not use advertising trackers, behavioral profiling, or third-party webfonts. The color-mode control stores one preference on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

Standard server and delivery logs may record technical request data such as an IP address, user agent, requested path, and timestamp. These logs are used for security, reliability, and aggregate traffic analysis, then retained according to the hosting provider's controls.

If we add a newsletter or other service that collects personal information, its purpose, provider, and retention terms will be disclosed before collection begins.

Questions about privacy can be sent to [contact@thecodelesstaken.com](mailto:contact@thecodelesstaken.com).
