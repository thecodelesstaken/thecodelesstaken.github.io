Each post begins with a current engineering problem and one overlooked mechanism. We publish when the mechanism clarifies an engineering decision or provides a technique readers can test.

## Evidence

Claims rely on the closest available sources: original documentation and code, published research, standards, or first-hand technical accounts. When implementation matters, we publish the relevant code, environment, versions, and limitations whenever practical.

## Comparison and judgment

We choose explicit criteria and a credible current baseline before reaching a conclusion. Low adoption does not prove that an idea was inferior, and obscurity does not make it superior. Published work distinguishes verified fact, interpretation, and unresolved questions.

## Review and corrections

Major technical and historical claims receive specialist review when qualified reviewers are available. Material corrections remain visible, and incomplete evidence remains identified as such.
