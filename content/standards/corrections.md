+++
title = "Corrections policy"
description = "How The Code Less Taken receives, evaluates, records, and publishes corrections."
+++

Accuracy matters more than preserving the first version of a claim. Send corrections to [contact@thecodelesstaken.com](mailto:contact@thecodelesstaken.com).

Please identify the page, the disputed passage, the proposed correction, and the strongest source available. Reports about broken builds or irreproducible results should include the platform, versions, commands, and relevant output.

Minor typographic fixes may be made without a formal notice. Changes that alter a technical claim, historical account, comparison, or conclusion will be recorded with the affected work. We will state what changed and, when useful, what evidence prompted the change.

Open disagreements will not be presented as settled. If the evidence remains incomplete, the page will say so.
